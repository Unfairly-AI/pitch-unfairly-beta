What operations leads ask before they sign.

Anything else, ask {{facts.contact.first}}. The answer goes on this page.

Do we have to change our booking tool?

No. Jobs keep coming in the way they do now, and {{facts.vendor.name}} reads them from there.

What if a dispatcher disagrees with a route?

They change it. The rest of the day re-plans around the change in a few seconds.

How do you count the time saved?

Drive time per job in the four weeks before go-live, against the four weeks after. You see the report.

Can we start with one crew?

Yes. Many teams start with one depot and add the rest after a month.

How are we billed?

Monthly, per active technician. A technician who doesn't log in that month isn't billed.
